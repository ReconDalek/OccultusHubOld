About Occultus

In the beginning there was only one faction.
It was dark and full of evil.

A group of rebels were given a chance of freedom by Lance the Lost one.
They stepped into the veil beyond and founded their own faction.
The faction was born into blood, fire and revenge.

The trolls of NNGO screamed their anger at the temerity of those who called themselves Occultus.
They spat vitriol and the rebel faction braced itself as it chose a path of quiet determination.
Other factions tried their mettle but found the rebel faction had something different.
It had a sense of value and respect.

Many times the faction was attacked but after each blood bath the faction grew and the bards started to sing of their exploits.
The trolls clawed at their own throats choking, as Occultus continued to thrive.
When word of their success reached them they were compelled to bounty or attempt to destroy their chains.
Occultus laughed as they drank their beer and ate their chocolate (for in those days many of them were still quite small)
They wiped the blood from their faces happy in the knowledge that one day they would see NNGO blackened in the fires of deceit and decay.

And so it would happen...

- Jackles [2683257]
